## III. Karpathy in Industry Analysis

### Positive Evaluations
1. **"Bridge figure in AI education"**: Key person connecting academic research and engineering practice
2. **"Successful transition from research to education"**: Chose D&E (Director of Engineering) over Research at OpenAI, widely seen as the best role for him
3. **"Prophet of Software 2.0"**: The 2017 "Software 2.0" essay is widely cited as a prophecy of AI engineering
4. **"Benchmark for open-source education"**: nanoGPT/llm.c/micrograd seen as best practices in open-source education

### Criticisms & Controversies
1. **"Not producing original research"**:
   - Some commentary suggests Karpathy's contributions are mainly in education and engineering integration, not original research breakthroughs
   - Counter: His PhD thesis (Deep Visual-Semantic Alignments) is a highly cited paper in image captioning (12,000+ citations)
   - **Credibility**: Second-hand, Medium

2. **"Ambiguity of Karpathy's role at OpenAI"**:
   - Some question whether Karpathy had enough research influence in early OpenAI
   - When Karpathy left in 2024, he only posted "the end" + a GIF, no detailed explanation
   - Some speculate he may have disagreed with OpenAI's strategic direction (but no solid evidence)
   - **Credibility**: Second-hand, Low (speculative)

3. **"Tesla-era Autopilot promotion"**:
   - Provided technical endorsement for Autopilot during his Tesla tenure, while Autopilot's safety record has been controversial
   - Karpathy himself focused more on technical details rather than safety debates
   - **Credibility**: Second-hand, Medium
