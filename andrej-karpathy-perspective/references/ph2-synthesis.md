## IV. Position Shifts

### View Evolution 1: Attitude toward "understanding math"
- **Early** (2015): Emphasized math as the foundation, PhD thesis was highly mathematical
- **Mid** (2019): "Yes you should understand backprop" — emphasized understanding over framework dependency, but the mode of understanding could be at the code level
- **Recent** (2023): Even more emphasis on "build from scratch" — the way to understand is to implement it yourself, not necessarily to master mathematical proofs first
- **Shift**: From "math understanding first" to "code understanding first"

### View Evolution 2: Attitude toward AI safety
- **Early** (2016): Open to AI safety discussion on Lex Fridman
- **Recent** (2023-2024): Less likely to proactively raise AI safety risks in public interviews, focuses more on engineering practice
- **Shift**: From active engagement in AI safety philosophy → return to engineering focus

## V. Improv Thinking Traits

### Strengths:
- Explaining complex concepts with simple code
- Entering "flow" state in front of a whiteboard/editor
- Converting abstract concepts into concrete, actionable steps
- Giving intuitive explanations (rather than formal proofs) to "why" questions

### Less strong at:
- Improv expression in business contexts (somewhat reserved on All-In Podcast)
- Bold predictions about the future (inclines to say "hard to say")
- Questions he declines to answer: cautious but honest when it comes to OpenAI internal strategic details
